BeatClip – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

BeatClip is a personal desktop application that runs on its owner's own computer. It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos and set their metadata (title, description, playlist) on the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data we access

With your permission, BeatClip uses Google OAuth to access your YouTube account in order to upload videos and manage playlists. The access token is stored only locally on your computer.

Data we store or share

BeatClip does not collect, sell, or share any personal data with third parties. No data is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API.

Revoking access

You can revoke BeatClip's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

BeatClip's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
